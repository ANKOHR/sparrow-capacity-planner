# Lessons for KOML

Synthetic evidence validates arithmetic and deterministic allocation. It does not validate the assumption that owners know their available capacity or job durations accurately, nor that the tool will earn daily use. Those assumptions remain untested until Henry's real trial.

Capacity is a budget within an explicit window, not a forecast. Overbooked commitments must be visible even when zero proposals fit. Sorting and indivisible-job rules materially affect which jobs fit; a large rejected job can be followed by an accepted smaller one.

Operating rule: define the units, planning window, allocation policy, and independent expected decisions before implementing a calculator. Ship input hashes and provenance with every result, and measure demand separately from test correctness.
